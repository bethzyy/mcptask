# Daoist State Concept Investigation (v42)

## Task
Find a Daoist philosophical concept that satisfies **ALL** conditions below.

## Candidate Concepts (investigate ALL of these)

| # | Concept | Pinyin | Translation |
|---|---------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | 无极 | Wuji | Limitless / Ultimate Nothingness |
| 2 | 太极 | Taiji | Supreme Ultimate |
| 3 | 虚 | Xu | Emptiness / Void |
| 4 | 清静 | Qingjing | Clarity and Stillness |
| 5 | 德 | De | Virtue / Power |
| 6 | 灵 | Ling | Spirit / Numinous |
| 7 | 静 | Jing | Stillness / Quietude |
| 8 | 恬 | Tian | Tranquility / Contentment |
| 9 | 淳 | Chun | Purity / Simplicity |
| 10 | 澹 | Dan | Serenity / Calmness |

## REQUIRED Conditions (ALL must be satisfied)

| # | Condition | Type |
|---|-----------|------|
| 1 | Describes a **STATE** (how things ARE), NOT an action/practice | Affirmative |
| 2 | Is **NOT** primarily a cosmological/metaphysical concept about the universe | Negative |
| 3 | Is mentioned in the **Tao Te Ching** (Daodejing) - must cite chapter number | Affirmative |
| 4 | Is associated with personal cultivation or meditation practice | Affirmative |
| 5 | Has at least one associated historical figure who wrote about it (must name the figure) | Affirmative |

## Interactive Investigation Process

### Phase 1: Wikipedia Search & Navigation
1. Use the Wikipedia **search box** to search for "Daoism" or "Taoism"
2. Navigate to the **Daoism** article
3. Use **internal links** within the article to find concept pages
4. For each candidate concept, use the **search function** to find its page
5. Navigate between related concept pages using **hyperlinks**

### Phase 2: Stanford Encyclopedia Interactions
1. Navigate to https://plato.stanford.edu/
2. Use the **search function** (top right corner) to search for "Taoism"
3. Click on the **"Taoism" entry** from the search results
4. Use the **Table of Contents** (left sidebar) to navigate to relevant sections
5. Click **cross-reference links** (blue underlined text) to explore related concepts
6. Use the **Related Entries** section at the bottom to find additional information

### Phase 3: Cross-Site Verification
1. Compare findings from Wikipedia and Stanford Encyclopedia
2. Verify the historical figure information across sources
3. Confirm the Tao Te Ching chapter citation
4. Use browser **back and forward navigation** to cross-reference

## Important Notes

- **Only ONE concept passes ALL five conditions** - you must investigate carefully
- Many concepts look similar but fail one or more conditions
- Some concepts are primarily cosmological (about the universe) rather than personal states
- Some concepts appear in later Daoist texts but NOT in the original Tao Te Ching
- Some concepts describe practices/actions rather than states of being
- Take your time to verify each condition carefully for each candidate
